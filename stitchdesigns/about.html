OUR
STORY

Born in the heart of Melbourne, Angel Archive began as a curated vision to bridge the gap between traditional South Asian artisanship and contemporary Western luxury.

As a premier Melbourne-based retailer, we specialize in luxury ethnic wear that transcends seasons. Our journey is one of preservation—archiving the techniques of master weavers and reimagining them for the modern global citizen. Every garment in our collection is a testament to the enduring beauty of heritage textiles.
